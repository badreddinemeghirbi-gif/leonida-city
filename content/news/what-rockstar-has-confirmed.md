Most of what circulates about Grand Theft Auto VI is guesswork. Some of it is reasonable guesswork, some of it is invented. This page tries to hold the line between the two.

## Confirmed: the setting

The game takes place in the state of **Leonida**, with **Vice City** as its urban centre. This is the series returning to its Florida-inspired setting for the first time in a very long while.

Leonida is a state, not just a city — which implies terrain beyond the urban core. Coastline, wetland, and smaller towns are all consistent with what has been shown.

## Confirmed: two protagonists

The game follows **Jason and Lucia**, a pair rather than a single lead. Dual and triple protagonists have appeared in the series before, but a partnership presented this way is a different structure, and it shapes expectations about how the story is told.

## Confirmed: platforms

PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Current-generation only. PC has not been dated.

## Not confirmed, despite what you'll read

- **Map size.** Comparisons to previous games circulate constantly with precise-sounding numbers attached. None of them come from Rockstar.
- **The online component.** How multiplayer works, when it launches, what it costs — none of this has been detailed.
- **Price.** Nothing official. Speculation about a price increase is just that.
- **Specific missions, vehicles, or mechanics.** Trailer footage shows moments, not systems.

## How to read GTA 6 news

A useful filter: ask where the claim originates. If the answer is "a video with a dramatic thumbnail" or "a post with no source," it's entertainment, not information. Rockstar communicates through its own channels, and anything significant will appear there first.

That's an unsatisfying answer when you want to know more. It's also the accurate one.
