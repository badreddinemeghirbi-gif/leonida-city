Rockstar has never been in a hurry, and Grand Theft Auto VI has been no exception. If you've lost track of where the release date actually stands, here's the whole picture in one place.

## Where it stands now

The game is currently slated for **19 November 2026** on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. PC has not been given a date.

That PC omission is worth dwelling on, because it follows a well-established pattern. Previous entries in the series have shipped on consoles first and arrived on PC considerably later — sometimes by more than a year. Nothing about this release suggests Rockstar has changed that approach.

## The pattern of delays

Large open-world games slip. That isn't cynicism, it's arithmetic: the bigger the world, the more systems have to work together, and the more places a schedule can come apart. Rockstar has historically chosen to delay rather than ship something unfinished, and by most accounts that has served the studio well.

What it means for you is simple. Treat any date as provisional until the game is actually in people's hands, and be sceptical of anyone claiming insider knowledge of a schedule change before Rockstar announces it.
